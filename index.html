What this system does

Submit one high-level business request. The Orchestrator decides which specialists are needed, what evidence is required, and what order the work runs in. Results pass Quality Assurance before you see them.

  • No invented market statistics or competitor figures
  • Evidence IDs and confidence labels on significant findings
  • Human approval gates for high-impact external actions
  • Designed to connect later to Marketing, Sales, Finance and other CINTEXA departments
